Read back, private days, and what I learned

Three choices. Pick a letter for each. Colours and sizes are the app's own. The font is still the plain system one because you haven't chosen a font yet. All the example text is made up.

1 · Read back: each part in its own section

Right now Read back is one long mixed list. You asked for each part to have its own section and to be very easy to see. Morning pages stay locked for 4 weeks.

2 · Keeping a day's answers private from Together

Your Mental health exercise and Daily check-in go to Oliver after 3am. That includes the date, which exercise or questions, and your answers. A tick keeps that day's answers here only. Shown on a finished check-in. The Mental health exercise would get the same tick.

Whichever you pick: once a day's answers have gone, ticking private hides them in Together but can't wipe them from Oliver's side. The app will say that plainly if you tick it after 3am.

3 · "What I learned today" in Mental health exercise

An optional box for what you learned about BPD or mental health that day. It goes to Oliver with that day's exercise (unless the day is private).
