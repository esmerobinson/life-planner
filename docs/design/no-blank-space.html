Making every page feel full

I screenshotted every page at Mac and phone size with made-up data. Most pages already use the full width. The blank space comes from four places, drawn below as small Mac windows. Pick a letter for each. The first option in each row, marked "now", is how it looks today.

I'll also do this without asking, since it's plainly what "full" means: text boxes (Add to inbox, Sync, Backup) stretch to the full width instead of stopping at about a fifth of it.

1 · Goals page: rows with empty slots

Each area has its own row of up to 3 cards. An area with one goal (Art, Body) leaves two-thirds of its row empty.

2 · Home: the goals column stops early

Today runs down the page, but "Goals this month" ends after three cards, leaving an empty block under it.

3 · Short pages that stop high up

Bin, Sync, Backup, Repeating and Sort one by one have little on them, so half the window is bare page below.

4 · Journal tabs on the Mac

On the Mac the five Journal tabs stack as five wide rows, each mostly empty. You chose "stack vertically, never scroll sideways". That still applies on the phone.
